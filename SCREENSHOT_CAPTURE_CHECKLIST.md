# Screenshot Capture Checklist (Sanitized Evidence)

Date: 2026-05-15  
Purpose: Capture realistic audit evidence without exposing personal or employer data.

## Required Screenshots

1. Access review table opened (`ACCESS_REVIEW_TABLE.md`)
2. Exception log opened (`EXCEPTION_LOG.md`)
3. Remediation tracker opened (`REMEDIATION_TRACKER.md`)
4. One example of an access reduction decision (Modify)
5. One example of an access removal decision (Remove)
6. Findings summary section in `FINDINGS.md`

## File Naming Convention

- `screenshots/01-access-review-table.png`
- `screenshots/02-exception-log.png`
- `screenshots/03-remediation-tracker.png`
- `screenshots/04-modify-example.png`
- `screenshots/05-remove-example.png`
- `screenshots/06-findings-summary.png`

## Redaction Requirements (Mandatory)

Do not publish screenshots with:

- Real full names
- Real email addresses
- Real usernames
- Device names or hostnames
- Tenant names
- Internal ticket numbers tied to real environments
- Employer names or logos (unless explicitly approved)

## Quick QA Before Publish

- Verify all screenshots are readable.
- Verify all sensitive fields are sanitized or blocked.
- Confirm file names match the naming convention.
- Confirm screenshots support the decisions documented in the tables.
